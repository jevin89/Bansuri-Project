Raag Hansdhwani

A pentatonic raag originating from Carnatic music and widely performed in Hindustani classical music.

Raag Information

Jati: Audav-Audav

Time: Evening

Notes Omitted: Ma and Dha

Aroha (Ascending)

Sa Re Ga Pa Ni Sa'

Avaroha (Descending)

Sa' Ni Pa Ga Re Sa

Pakad (Characteristic Phrase)

Ga Re Sa, Ni Sa Re Ga, Pa Ga Re Sa

About Raag Hansdhwani

Raag Hansdhwani is a five-note raag known for its bright and uplifting character. It omits Ma and Dha.
