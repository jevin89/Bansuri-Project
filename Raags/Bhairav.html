Raag Bhairav

A traditional morning raag known for its serious and devotional character.

Raag Information

Thaat: Bhairav

Time: Early morning

Jati: Sampurna-Sampurna

Important Notes: Komal Re and Komal Dha

Aroha (Ascending)

Sa re(komal) Ga Ma Pa dha(komal) Ni Sa'

Avaroha (Descending)

Sa' Ni dha(komal) Pa Ma Ga re(komal) Sa

Pakad (Characteristic Phrase)

Ga Ma re(komal) Sa, Ni Sa

About Raag Bhairav

Raag Bhairav is traditionally performed in the early morning. It features komal Re and komal Dha, which are often rendered with slow oscillations.
